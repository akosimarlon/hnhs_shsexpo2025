PAHA CLUSTER

The PAHA Cluster (Padada-Hagonoy) brings together four dynamic secondary schools (Padada National High School, Hagonoy National High School, Lapulabao National High School, and Sacub National High School) united in their mission to deliver quality senior high school education in Davao del Sur.

Through collaboration, shared expertise, and community-driven initiatives, the cluster aims to empower learners with meaningful academic, technical, and real-world experiences that prepare them for college, careers, and lifelong success.
